Quick Overview

Killing Floor 3 is the third Entry in the Killing Floor series, a co-op horde shooter. But instead of zombies it has clones called Zeds. 3 is a noticeable improvement over 2 gameplay wise. And no, there are no collectable trophies this time around.

Difficulty Rating
Singleplayer 5/10 Multiplayer 9/10 Due to difficulty scaling.
Online/Offline Trophies
This is an Online only game and crossplay with PC is supported.

Platinum

The platinum, earn all other base game trophies.

Spread the Wealth

There are 2 ways to do this, either in trader pod press X on someone's name on the left side of the menu or outside of the menu hold down on the d pad to give away dosh. The tracking is broken for this trophy.

For this you need to be the Field Medic, you cannot change classes during a match. Press R1 while aiming at an ally to heal them. You can also heal them using your ultimate with L1 + R1.
