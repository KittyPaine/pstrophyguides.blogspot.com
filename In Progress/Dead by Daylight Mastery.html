Survivor Mastery & Mechanics

Mastering survivor play requires spacing geometry, resource management, and knowing precisely when to trade body health states or abandon a generator.

General Looping & Killer Evasion Guide

  • Line-of-Sight Breaking: Use tall loops (shacks, jungle gyms) to force mind-games. Keep your camera locked on the killer while running forward to track red-stain projections.
  • Pallet Economy: Never drop early out of panic. Match the killer's distance; force them to respect the pallet or take a stun. Dead zones are death traps—plan your rotations to adjacent safe loops.
  • The Healing Dilemma: Avoid healing immediately after unhooks if the killer has sloppy/mangled tracking or pressure is active. Heal when generators are safe, or when you can trade efficiency for a guaranteed hit-absorption state.

The Trapper Speed: 4.6m/s · Difficulty: 1/3

Power: Bear Traps
Expected Role: Area Denial, Loop Shut Down

Power Mechanics & Execution

Collect traps across the map and place them in high-traffic grass corridors, vault choke points, and under un-dropped pallet zones. Force survivors into positioning errors during chases.

Macro Pressure & Chase Commitment Rules

  • When to Give Up a Chase: If a survivor leads you away from trapped core loops or wastes excessive time trying to bait you into pre-dropped pallets, drop the chase immediately and return to patrol map generators.
  • Trap Reset Timing: Never spend entire phases walking across the map to reset a single trap. Reset traps organically as you rotate between pressured generators.

Optimal Add-Ons

Top-Tier Pairing: Honing Stone (Survivors freeing themselves from traps are automatically put into the dying state) + Tar Bottle (Darkens traps to make them nearly invisible in shadows and grass).
Alternative / Budget Utility: Setting Tools (Increases trap pickup and setting speed) for faster mobile setup pressure.
