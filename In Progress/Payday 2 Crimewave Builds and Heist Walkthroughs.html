A comprehensive, text-based tactical manual and completionist walkthrough suite. Designed for mobile offline APK deployment and high-efficiency achievement hunting.

1. Builds & Loadout Frameworks

Optimized skill point distributions, perk decks, and weapon setups tailored for high-difficulty progression, solo execution, and seamless stealth/loud transitions.

Stealth Utility Build (Ghost / Shinobi Focus)

Core Perk Deck: Burglar (or Hacker for portable ECM utility)

  • Primary Weapon: Compact/Silent weapon with low concealment (e.g., Cavity 9mm or Judge Shotgun with Silencer).
  • Deployable: ECM Jammer (Aced for feedback loops and pager blocking) or Body Bag Case.
  • Key Skills: Nimble Aced for safe/lockpicking speed, Cleaner Aced for body bag capacity, ECM Overdrive.

Loud Sustain Build (Armorer / Stoic Focus)

Core Perk Deck: Stoic or Armorer

  • Primary / Secondary: High-DPS LMG or Assault Rifle paired with a high-damage pocket shotgun (Judge) or SMG.
  • Deployable: Doctor Bags or First Aid Kits (depending on perk deck synergy).
  • Key Skills: Inspire Aced for clutch crew revives, Nine Lives Aced, heavy armor tier mastery.
