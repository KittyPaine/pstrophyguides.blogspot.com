Living Nightmare

How to Unlock Torment 2 in Diablo IV

  1. Reach Level 70: Level your character to 70 on the Eternal Realm. The Pit unlocks automatically once you hit level 70, and runs completed before this level will not count toward difficulty unlocks.
  2. Unlock Torment 1: Gather 3 Artificer's Stones and interact with the Artificer's Obelisk in Cerrigar or Temis. Complete Pit Tier 10 within the 15-minute time limit to unlock Torment 1.
  3. Clear Pit Tier 15: Spend another 3 Artificer's Stones at the obelisk and complete Pit Tier 15 before the 15-minute timer expires to unlock Torment 2.
  4. Switch to Torment 2: Visit the World Tier statue in any major town and change your game difficulty to Torment 2.
  5. Obtain a Nightmare Sigil: Earn a Nightmare Sigil from Tree of Whispers caches, Helltides, or World Bosses, or craft one directly at the Occultist for 60 Sigil Powder and 4,000 Gold.
  6. Complete the Nightmare Dungeon: Activate the Nightmare Sigil from your Dungeon Keys tab, teleport to the dungeon, and clear it solo to unlock the trophy. (Note: Co-op completions on Torment 2 have been reported to occasionally prevent the trophy from popping. If it doesn't unlock on Torment 2, clear Pit Tier 20 and run one on Torment 3.)

Turning the Tides

The Hell tide is a specific area of the map that is marked red by the Hell tide. Enter this area and kill enemies to collect Aberrant Cinders which can then be used to unlock hell tide exclusive chests. These cinders disappear when the hell tide ends and relocates. You can use this website to track it's movement as well as chest locations.

Chaotic Whispers

These caches are extremely rare and will take a long time if done legitimately. However there is an exploit you can use. Drop the cache repeatedly and pick it up. This counts towards the trophy. As far as I know this has not been patched.

Tormented Massacre Rogue Death Trap build
  • Stack Pre-Farm XP Buffs: Always maintain an active Elixir (+8% XP) and Incense (+5% XP). Before starting a session, run a Nightmare Dungeon with the Forgotten Wisdom affix to click the Fountain of Wisdom for a 1-hour +15% XP buff.
  • Speedfarm Kurast Undercity (With Tributes): Whenever you have Tributes of Growth, run the Kurast Undercity on the highest Torment tier you can clear rapidly. Aim for maximum Attunement before killing the boss to trigger the largest end-of-run XP bonus.
  • Run 60-90 Second Pit Speedruns (Sustained Loop): Once out of Tributes of Growth, speed-farm The Pit. Drop down to a Pit tier your build can clear in under 90 seconds without stopping, as rapid flat completion bonuses yield more XP per hour than slow high-tier pushes.
  • Chain Helltides and Whisper Caches: Farm high-density Helltide zones while completing overlapping Tree of Whispers objectives. Turning in Whisper caches grants chunks of completion XP alongside extra Tributes and Nightmare Sigils.
  • Farm 10-Wave Infernal Hordes or Lair Bosses: Run 10-wave Infernal Hordes in a group for dense, stationary elite spawns, or speed-kill Urivar, Duriel, and Andariel in rotations if you have summoning materials.
  • Unlock War Plans & Stack XP Buffs: Finish the Lord of Hatred campaign and speak with Tyrael in Temis to unlock the War Plans Command Table. Keep your Forgotten Wisdom dungeon well buff (+15% XP), Elixir, and Incense active.
  • Speedfarm Kurast Undercity with Jade Epiphany (Best Solo Method): Spec into the Jade Epiphany node on the Kurast Undercity War Plan skill tree so every Attunement rank spawns clusters of physical XP orbs. Combine this with Tributes of Growth and XP-boosting Charms for massive scaling XP.
  • Loop Helltide Hellwyrms at Threat Level 3: Invest points into Hellmouth and Writhe and Rot in your Helltide War Plan tree. Build your Helltide Threat meter to max, stop killing random trash mobs so the meter holds, and loop between Hellwyrm spawns to farm maggot waves that burst into XP orbs.
  • Farm Nightmare Dungeons with Horror Drake Hunter: Spec into Horror Drake Hunter on the Nightmare Dungeon War Plan tree to spawn a Horror Drake Altar every 25 elite kills, summoning extra elite packs on demand.
  • Run Torment 12 Infernal Hordes (Best Group Method): Run 10-wave Infernal Hordes in a coordinated party on Torment 12, prioritizing Chaos, Goblin, and Aether Lord waves for the highest group XP per minute in the game.

Hireling Commander

During the main campaign you will meet Raheir and unlock The Den. From there you can accept side quests to unlock the remaining mercenaries. If you do not get them to X before the season ends they may be boosted to X by the patch. Thankfully the trophy is attached to in game challenges and will pop once all 4 are completed. Simple level them all up once and it will pop, no need to start over.

Kurast Cleanser

You must complete the campaign to unlock Reunion. It has you doing tasks for the King of Kurast and his Vizier.


Devout Champion

The fastest ways to reach Paragon 300 with only the first expansion are Kurast Undercity with Tributes of Growth, Infernal Hordes, and 60-to-90 second Pit speedruns. With access to all DLC content, the fastest way to reach Paragon 300 is speedfarming the Kurast Undercity using War Plans and Tributes of Growth. Tributes of Growth are random drops found across Sanctuary once you unlock the Kurast Undercity. You unlock War Plans by finishing the Lord of Hatred expansion campaign and completing the The War for Sanctuary priority quest in Temis. Once unlocked, you access the system at the War Plans Command Table standing right next to Tyrael in Temis.

Adept Angler

The easiest way to get this is get the 20 fiah exclusive to Skovos which can all be caught in Shi Yugong's Haven. You have to consume them for them to count as collected. Fishing is a minigame requiring a button press to catch the fish when they bite, the better your timing the better your chance at a rarer fish. Look for bubbles these also have a chance for rare fish. Sometimes changing the spot you are fishing at can improve your luck. Fishing is done using the emote wheel.
